# Decision log

Short, dated entries. One decision per entry. Say why, so it is not relitigated blindly. Do not edit old entries: add a new one that supersedes.

## Template

### [YYYY-MM-DD]: [decision in one line]
- **Decided by**: [user | agent with user approval]
- **Why**: [the reason, in one or two sentences, including the main alternative rejected]
- **Affects**: [specs, stories, platforms, files]
- **Revisit if**: [condition, or "no"]
- **Supersedes**: [earlier entry date and title, or none]
