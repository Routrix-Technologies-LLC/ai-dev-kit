# For AI assistants

This repository is the AI Dev Kit. If the person asked you to install it, follow [AI-INSTALL.md](AI-INSTALL.md) step by step.

If you were asked to work on the kit itself: the installer is `install.py` (standard library only), the standing instructions are `kit/instructions.md`, the kit skills are in `kit/skills/`, and the teams are in `teams/`. The teams are generated from a maintained library, so changes made here may be replaced by the next release.
